FAQ

Before you begin.

Will this guarantee weight loss?

No. Results vary by person and depend on many factors. The service focuses on practical nutrition and lifestyle habits, not guaranteed outcomes.

Is this a medical or therapeutic diet?

No. This website offers general nutrition and wellness guidance. People with medical conditions or those needing therapeutic nutrition should consult a qualified registered dietitian or doctor.

Can I eat normal Indian food?

Yes. The approach is designed around practical, familiar foods and sensible meal structure rather than unnecessary restriction.
